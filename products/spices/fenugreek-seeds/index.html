About Indian Fenugreek Seeds

Fenugreek — methi dana — is the dried seed of Trigonella foenum-graecum, an annual legume valued both as a culinary spice and as a source of bioactive compounds used in pharmaceutical and nutraceutical manufacturing. India is one of the world's largest producers, with Rajasthan alone accounting for over 80% of national output, followed by Gujarat, Madhya Pradesh, and Uttarakhand. Within Rajasthan, the Kota and Jhalawar mandis set the benchmark spot price that the broader trade references.

Beyond its use as a spice — with its distinctive slightly bitter, nutty, maple-like flavour — fenugreek is significant to pharmaceutical buyers for its diosgenin content, a steroidal saponin compound used as a precursor in pharmaceutical synthesis, alongside trigonelline and galactomannan, which drive demand from the wellness and functional-food sector.

Nexus Global Exim supplies fenugreek seed in Sortex-cleaned and organic-certified grades, whole or ground. Note for EU-bound buyers: the EU enforces strict zero-tolerance rules on Ethylene Oxide (ETO) residue in fenugreek, so we ensure steam sterilisation (not ETO fumigation) with clean lab reports for any shipment destined for European markets.
