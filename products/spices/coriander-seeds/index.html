About Indian Coriander Seeds

Coriander seeds — dhaniya — are the dried fruit of Coriandrum sativum, a plant whose leaves (cilantro) and seeds are both widely traded but used quite differently: the seed carries a warm, citrusy, slightly sweet flavour distinct from the leaf's fresh, herbaceous character. India, Russia, and Ukraine together account for roughly 80% of global coriander seed exports, with India's main growing belts centred on Rajasthan and Madhya Pradesh.

Nexus Global Exim supplies coriander across the three standard export grades. Eagle grade is the premium tier — large, whole, plump, greenish-yellow seeds with high volatile oil content, sought after for whole-seed retail and premium spice blends. Scooter grade is the trade's workhorse — a more affordable, slightly split, yellowish-brown seed used predominantly for grinding into coriander powder. Parrot grade (single and double) sits below Scooter, suited to bulk applications where cost matters more than whole-seed appearance.

We supply whole, split, and powdered coriander, Sortex-processed for colour and purity, with lab testing for moisture and volatile oil content on every export lot. Based on recent trade data, the United States, Canada, and the Philippines are consistently among the top importers of Indian coriander seed.
