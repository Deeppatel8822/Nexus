Certified & compliant

Certified quality. Documented every shipment.

Every Nexus Global Exim shipment comes with the certifications your buyers and destination countries require. No shortcuts, no delays.

Company registrations

Our licences & registrations

🏛
IEC — Import Export Code
Mandatory DGFT registration for all Indian exporters. Our foundational export licence.
🌶
Spices Board Registration
Registered exporter with the Spices Board of India. Mandatory for all spice exports.
⚗️
Chemexcil Registration
Basic Chemicals Export Promotion Council member for chemical exports from India.
📋
GSTIN Registration
GST-registered company. IGST refund claimed on all zero-rated export supplies.
Shipment documents

Documents provided with every shipment

📜
Spices Board Export Certificate
Mandatory for all spice exports. Issued by Spices Board of India. Accepted globally.
🌿
Phytosanitary Certificate
Issued by Plant Quarantine Dept. Certifies product is free of pests and plant diseases.
🔬
Lab Analysis Report
NABL-accredited lab tests for pesticide residue, aflatoxin, moisture and purity.
🌍
Certificate of Origin (COO)
Issued by DGFT or Chamber of Commerce. Enables GSP preferential tariff benefits.
🚢
Bill of Lading
Issued by shipping line after loading. Original BL sent via courier or telex release.
📋
COA & MSDS (Chemicals)
Certificate of Analysis and Material Safety Data Sheet included with all chemical shipments.
