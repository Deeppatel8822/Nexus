About Indian Fennel Seeds

Fennel seeds — saunf — are the dried fruit of Foeniculum vulgare, native to the Mediterranean but now grown extensively in India, particularly across Rajasthan, Gujarat, Punjab, Uttar Pradesh, and Madhya Pradesh as a Rabi (winter) crop. The seed's distinctive sweet, anise-like flavour comes primarily from anethole, its dominant aromatic compound — the same molecule responsible for the flavour of star anise and liquorice, which is why fennel and anise are often paired or substituted in recipes and formulations.

Nexus Global Exim supplies two benchmark Indian varieties. Lucknowi fennel — grown around Rajasthan and Uttar Pradesh — is the premium, higher-priced grade favoured by Europe, the Middle East, and the USA for its superior aroma and bright green colour. Jaipuri fennel is the cost-effective bulk option, well suited to large-scale food processing and mid-range spice blend manufacturing. Within both varieties, seeds are further graded by size — Extra Bold, Bold, Standard, and Small/Grinding quality — with larger, brighter-green seeds commanding the highest prices.

Every lot is Sortex-cleaned and colour-sorted, with the option of organic certification (NPOP, EU, USDA) for buyers in the premium wellness and herbal tea segments, where fennel has strong demand as a digestive aid ingredient.
